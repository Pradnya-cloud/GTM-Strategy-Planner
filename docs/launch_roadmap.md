# Launch Roadmap

## Pre-Launch
Research, positioning, pricing, landing page, campaign preparation, sales enablement.

## Launch
Campaign activation, demos, trial, PR, partnerships, lead generation.

## Post-Launch
Customer feedback, adoption analysis, conversion optimization, retention, roadmap updates.

## 30-60-90
Days 1–30: preparation and validation.
Days 31–60: launch and acquisition.
Days 61–90: optimization, retention and scaling.
