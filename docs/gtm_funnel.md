# GTM Funnel

Awareness → Interest → Lead → Qualified Lead → Demo/Trial → Conversion → Activation → Retention

| Stage | Objective | KPI | Customer Action | Business Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Reach ICP | Reach | Sees content | Distribute content |
| Interest | Build relevance | Engagement | Visits site | Demonstrate use cases |
| Lead | Capture demand | Leads | Signs up | Capture intent |
| Qualified Lead | Prioritize fit | MQL/SQL | Confirms need | Score leads |
| Demo/Trial | Prove value | Trial starts | Uses planner | Guide onboarding |
| Conversion | Monetize | CVR | Purchases | Offer right plan |
| Activation | Reach value | Activation | Creates plan | Trigger success |
| Retention | Sustain value | Retention | Reuses product | Support/expand |
