# Go-To-Market Strategy Planner for New Product Launch

## Overview
A job-ready Product Management and GTM analytics project for **LaunchPilot AI**, an AI SaaS product connecting market research, customer segmentation, positioning, pricing, channels, funnel analytics, revenue forecasting, launch readiness and risk management.

## Business Problem
Strong products can fail when teams target the wrong customers, use weak positioning, misprice the offer, select inefficient channels or launch without readiness gates.

## Objective
**MARKET → CUSTOMER → POSITIONING → CHANNEL → ACQUISITION → CONVERSION → REVENUE → LAUNCH → GROWTH**

## Product Scenario
LaunchPilot AI targets SaaS startups, technology SMEs, product teams, growth teams and consulting organizations.

## Market Sizing
TAM $24.0M · SAM $8.4M · SOM $0.84M.

## Positioning
For product and growth teams with fragmented GTM planning, LaunchPilot AI provides one integrated workflow for market, customer, channel, revenue and launch-readiness decisions.

## Value Proposition
FOR product and growth teams WHO have fragmented GTM planning, OUR PRODUCT provides an AI-assisted launch planning workflow, UNLIKE spreadsheets and disconnected tools, WE connect market, customer, channel, funnel, revenue and readiness decisions.

## Pricing
Starter $49/mo · Growth $99/mo · Scale $199/mo.

## Channels
Prioritize LinkedIn, webinars, SEO, email and partnerships; use Google Ads for high-intent demand.

## Funnel
Awareness → Interest → Lead → Qualified Lead → Demo/Trial → Conversion → Activation → Retention.

## Revenue
Base: $11,880 monthly / $35,640 quarterly / $142,560 annualized.

## Launch Readiness
Evaluate product, engineering, sales, marketing, support, legal/compliance and operations before scaling.

## Risk Analysis
Track demand, pricing, positioning, bugs, competitive response, conversion, CAC, onboarding, retention and sales readiness.

## 30-60-90 Plan
1–30: validate ICP, positioning, pricing and readiness.
31–60: launch channels, trials and demos.
61–90: optimize CAC, activation, retention and scale winners.

## Dashboard
Open `dashboard/index.html` in a browser. It contains KPI cards, TAM-SAM-SOM, segment analysis, competitor benchmark, pricing, channel performance, funnel, revenue forecast, readiness, risk matrix, roadmap and recommendations.

## Strategic Recommendations
1. Start with B2B SaaS product/growth teams.
2. Lead with LinkedIn, webinars, SEO and partnerships.
3. Use tiered pricing plus a trial.
4. Scale only after CAC, activation and retention gates are met.
5. Differentiate on integrated GTM planning.

## Future Scope
CRM/product analytics integrations, live ad data, AI-generated messaging, experiment tracking, cohort retention, scenario modeling and collaboration.
